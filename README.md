#THIS IS A STORY ABOUT A PENGUIN

Hello my name is Pinguy I am a penguin, I live in Antarctica
my favourite thing to do is to eat fish while watching the ocean, I find it very relaxing
I like to cook my fish by deep frying it and then when it is golden brown I add barbecue sauce and I eat it in one go

Then, after savoring that crunchy and tasty bite, I always feel ready to dive into the icy waters.
I love the rolling waves and the sound of icebergs cracking in the distance.
It gives me incredible energy for my next fishing adventure.
